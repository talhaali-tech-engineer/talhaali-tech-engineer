### [MedShelf](https://github.com/talhaali-tech-engineer/MedShelf)

Offline-first medicine stock and expiry control for small pharmacies and clinic dispensaries.

- Batch-level stock with first-expiry-first-out issuing, expiry alerts and reorder advice
- Shows which batches are likely to expire before they are used, and what they are worth, while there is still time to act
- Optional AI invoice scanning (OpenAI or Claude); nothing is saved until staff have checked every line
- No build step and no dependencies; tested with GitHub Actions and deployed on GitHub Pages

### [DampLog](https://github.com/talhaali-tech-engineer/DampLog)

An evidence log for damp, mould and repairs in rented homes in England, with Awaab's Law deadline tracking.

- Dated photos, notes, calls and visits in one timeline for each problem
- Works out every deadline the landlord must meet in working days, bank holidays included, and shows when they are late
- Builds letters (report, chase, formal complaint) and a printable evidence report from the record
- Optional AI describes photos and tightens letters; every change is shown, and code checks that no date, reference or name was lost or invented

[**Live app**](https://talhaali-tech-engineer.github.io/DampLog/) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Source code](https://github.com/talhaali-tech-engineer/DampLog)
